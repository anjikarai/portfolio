Clinic Call

2026

AI voice agent that phones clinics to reschedule appointments

An outbound caller that holds a real phone conversation. Call audio streams over Twilio as 8 kHz μ-law, runs through Deepgram speech-to-text with turn detection, a Groq-hosted LLM, and Deepgram text-to-speech, then back into the call. A React dashboard registers clinics, collects availability, and shows calls and transcripts live.

Illustrative script written for this page, not a recorded call. Each step lights up as the agent listens and replies.
